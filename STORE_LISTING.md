# Riots: Kevlar Edition

Store name / command: `riots-kevlar`

## Short description

Fight a robot outbreak with your AI squad.

## Description

Land by helicopter, scavenge equipment and lead your squad through a ruined world overrun by robots. Explore scrapyards and mountain terrain, take the wheel of ground vehicles, fight rival AI squads and complete field contracts.

Start with Normal operations and earn levels to unlock Solo, Guerilla and Knife-Only. Spend earned Scrap on attachments, Kevlar on clothing and Platinum on weapons. Preview your equipment on the gunsmith workbench and customize your operator.

Riots: Kevlar Edition is an independent, offline single-player game. All squadmates and opponents are AI-controlled. It has no online multiplayer, cloud saves or real-money purchases. Local profiles save progress on your computer. Contains fictional weapon combat, blood and death reactions.

Developed with substantial AI assistance in code, packaging, documentation and original asset generation. The game combines locally authored assets with licensed third-party models, textures and sound recordings. Credits accompany every package.

## Requirements and scope

- Linux x86_64; keyboard and mouse.
- Default renderer: Vulkan. Compatibility rendering is available via `--rendering-method gl_compatibility`.
- Tested locally on an NVIDIA GTX 1650. No universal frame-rate or minimum hardware guarantee has been established.
- Local profile storage persists across package updates; Flatpak and Snap have separate save locations.
- Release includes the normal game only; admin and Broken Horizon are separate products.
